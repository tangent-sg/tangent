**Subject:** Tangent: a non-VC incubator in Singapore, and how you could help

---

I wanted to make sure you know about Tangent, and to ask whether you'd be open to supporting it.

Tangent is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore. It's sector-agnostic and doesn't select on demographics or credentials. It selects on one thing above all: **a credible path to free cash flow, fast**, ideally within 12 months.

**We're the complement to venture capital, not a version of it.** VC funds businesses chasing very large, uncertain markets or new technologies. Tangent looks for the inverse: real problems in existing markets, solved with well-understood technology, by founders who want to be self-sustaining early and are fine with staying small. They build using AI and technology, even when they aren't building AI products. That lets us back for-profits, social enterprises, not-for-profits and creative practices alike.

**It's working.** Each cohort is a four-week, in-person sprint. Teams get a discretionary tooling budget, AI tools and a place to work. Tangent takes no equity. Our first cohort ran in July and August 2026 with 17 participants across 10 projects, and four teams (LateranAI, Solvere, Opine and Mare) had contracted revenue by the end of the four weeks. We have eight cohorts scheduled through the end of 2027.

**Where we need help:**
- **Funding.** Our current funding covers three of the eight scheduled cohorts. Support goes directly to running the programme: team tooling budgets, tools and subscriptions, financial support for participants who need it, and cohort costs.
- **Space.** Our temporary space runs to the end of October 2026. We need about 1,100 sq ft to run cohorts, or 2,000–2,500 sq ft for an alumni and founder community, at a discounted or sponsored rate. In return we bring programming: workshops, drop-in coworking, and sessions co-run with the host's own audience.
- **Tools.** Donated or subsidised API credits, subscriptions or hardware, so teams can build without worrying about costs.
- **Your time.** Drop by the space during a cohort. Teams working on a problem in your domain will come and talk to you. Our low-volume guides WhatsApp group shares cohort dates and where we are: [WhatsApp group link].
- **Institutional cohorts.** We can run paid Tangent-model cohorts for a polytechnic, university, foundation or company.

There's more detail in our partners and donors page (https://tangent-sg.github.io/tangent/for-partners-and-donors/), on what we look for (https://tangent.org.sg/wishlist), in Tangent's Straits Times op-ed (https://vaughntan.org/techmittelstand), and in our incubation thesis (https://vaughntan.org/tangentlogic).

Tangent is the operating name of Go Tangent Ltd, a Singapore company limited by guarantee, funded by private philanthropy through the Majurity Trust. It's run by Dr Vaughn Tan (executive director: Fellow at the Centre for Strategic Futures, ex-Google, honorary associate professor at UCL) and Harper Chew (programme associate).
