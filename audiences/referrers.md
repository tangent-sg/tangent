**Subject:** Know someone building something that could pay for itself?

---

I wanted to make sure you know about Tangent, and to ask for your help finding the right people for it.

Tangent (https://tangent.org.sg) is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore. Each cohort is a four-week, in-person sprint, run several times a year around university term breaks. Teams get desk space, a discretionary tooling budget, AI tools, workshops on building fast-to-free-cash-flow businesses, and on-demand access to people with industry experience. Financial support is available for anyone who couldn't otherwise take four weeks out. There's no curriculum and no assignments. The only obligation is to share back what they did at the end, and Tangent takes no equity.

**What we look for is the idea, not a profile.** We're sector-agnostic and don't select on grades, credentials or background. We look for:
- a real problem in an existing market, solved with technology that already works
- a credible path to cash flow fast, whether from customers, contracts, grants or philanthropy
- excitement about using AI tools to build and run the business (it doesn't have to be an AI product)

We want realist founders who are okay with staying small, rather than people chasing a VC-scale unicorn. We select mainly on motivation and self-direction, and no technical background or prior experience is needed. The right person is often already building something without being asked to, and keeps coming back to the same problem. Social enterprises, not-for-profits and creative practices are as welcome as companies. Our full wishlist is at https://tangent.org.sg/wishlist.

**Why it works.** Tangent isn't a VC incubator. We back people who want sustainable revenue soon rather than a unicorn later. Our first cohort (17 participants, 10 projects) ran in July and August 2026, and four of its teams had contracted revenue by the end of the four weeks. You can see past participants at https://tangent.org.sg/cohorts.

How you can help:

2. **Pass this on.** Forward this to people who might know someone who fits.
3. **Drop by.** If you have industry experience, come by the space during a cohort. Teams will come to you if your experience is useful to them. To hear when cohorts are running, join our low-volume guides WhatsApp group: [WhatsApp group link].
