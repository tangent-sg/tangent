**Subject:** Four weeks to build something that pays for itself

---

I wanted to make sure you know about a programme I think might be right for you, or for someone you know.

Tangent is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore for realist founders: people who want to build something of their own that makes money and gets profitable fast, and who are okay with staying small. It isn't for founders chasing a VC-scale, deep-tech or massive-market unicorn. That can be a business, a social enterprise, a not-for-profit or a creative practice, in any sector. What we care about is whether it's designed to get to free cash flow quickly.

**What we look for:**
- **A real problem in an existing market**, for a specific group of people, using technology that already works. Not a speculative new market, and not a technology that still has to be invented.
- **A path to self-sustaining early.** Customers paying, a government contract, grants or philanthropic funding, plus reasoned choices about who to serve first, what to charge, and what to build now versus later.
- **Excited about AI tools.** You don't have to build an AI product, but you should be interested in using AI to build and run your business faster, more effectively and more cheaply.

More on this at https://tangent.org.sg/wishlist.

**What you get:** a four-week, in-person sprint. Each team gets:
- A discretionary tooling budget of S$800, plus S$200 per founder (for up to two founders)
- Four weeks of desk space and access to AI tools
- Workshops on building fast-to-free-cash-flow businesses
- On-demand access to people with industry experience
- Financial support, if you couldn't otherwise do four weeks in person

There's no fixed curriculum and no mandatory assignments. You decide what you need to figure out, and we help you do it. At the end of the four weeks you share back what you did, and that's the only obligation. Tangent takes no equity and charges no fees, so you leave owing nothing.

It works: four teams from our first cohort had contracted revenue by the end of their four weeks. You can see who's been through Tangent at https://tangent.org.sg/cohorts.

You don't need grades, pedigree, a technical background or prior experience. We select mainly on motivation and self-direction. Teams can have up to three people, and cohorts run several times a year around university term breaks.

Dates are at https://tangent.org.sg, and you can apply at https://tangent.org.sg/apply.
